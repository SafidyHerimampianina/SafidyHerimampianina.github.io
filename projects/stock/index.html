PO-BOT — automatic supplier purchase orders

A bot that automatically emails a purchase order to the supplier as soon as a product reaches its minimum stock.

Context

Restocking relied on teams tracking inventory by hand.

Problem

Manual tracking led to missed orders and stock-outs.

Solution & architecture

  • A minimum stock level set for each product
  • A purchase order email sent automatically to the supplier as soon as the minimum is reached
  • A small back-office to configure the emails that are sent
  • A history of sent emails, searchable by supplier

Stack

  • PHP
  • MySQL
  • JavaScript
  • Bootstrap

My role

End-to-end design and development.

Outcome

Automated restocking that frees teams from repetitive tracking work.
